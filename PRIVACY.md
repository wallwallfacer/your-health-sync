# Privacy statement: Your Health Sync

_Last updated: 2026-11-23_

**What it reads.** With your authorization, the app reads your weight, body fat rate, basal metabolic rate, bone mass and body water mass from HUAWEI Health, through HUAWEI Health Kit.

**What it writes.** The same measurements, written to Android Health Connect on your phone.

**Where data goes.** Nowhere else. The app has no server, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs besides HUAWEI Health Kit and Health Connect. Data moves only between those two services on your device. Nothing is transferred across borders by this app.

**Your control.**
- Revoke HUAWEI access in the app with **Revoke HUAWEI Health access**.
- Remove the app's Health Connect permissions and data in Health Connect settings → App permissions → Your Health Sync.
- Uninstall the app at any time.

**Children.** The app is not intended for children.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/wallwallfacer/your-health-sync/issues

# Privacy statement / 隐私声明: Your Health Sync

_Last updated / 更新日期: 2026-11-23_

**Developer / 开发者:** 徐涛 (individual developer / 个人开发者)
**App / 应用:** Your Health Sync (registered with HUAWEI as `your-health-sync`, package `io.github.wallwallfacer.yourhealthsync`)

## English

**What it reads.** With your authorization, the app reads these data types from HUAWEI Health, through HUAWEI Health Kit (read only):
- Weight and body composition (height & weight / body fat data): weight, body fat rate, basal metabolic rate, bone mass and body water mass.
- Historical data of up to 1 year, only when you tap **Backfill history (last year)**, so that past weigh-ins are copied once.

**Why.** The only purpose is to copy your own measurements to Android Health Connect on the same phone, so other health apps you choose (for example Samsung Health) can show them. The app does not analyze, profile or share your data.

**What it writes.** The same measurements, written to Android Health Connect on your phone.

**Where data goes.** Nowhere else. The app has no server, no account, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs besides HUAWEI Health Kit and Health Connect. Data moves only between those two services on your device. The developer never receives your data. Nothing is transferred across borders by this app.

**Storage.** The app keeps only the time and result of the last sync on the device. It does not keep copies of your measurements.

**Your control.**
- Revoke HUAWEI access in the app with **Revoke HUAWEI Health access**.
- Remove the app's Health Connect permissions and data in Health Connect settings → App permissions → Your Health Sync.
- Uninstall the app at any time.

**Children.** The app is not intended for children.

**Changes.** Changes to this statement are published in this file.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/wallwallfacer/your-health-sync/issues

## 中文

**读取哪些数据。** 经您授权，本应用通过 HUAWEI Health Kit 从华为运动健康读取以下数据（只读）：
- 体重及身体成分（身高体重/体脂数据）：体重、体脂率、基础代谢率、骨盐量、水分含量。
- 最长 1 年的历史数据，仅在您点击 **Backfill history (last year)**（回填历史数据）时读取，用于一次性复制过去的测量记录。

**用途。** 唯一用途是将您本人的测量数据复制到同一手机上的 Android Health Connect，以便您选择的其他健康应用（例如三星健康）显示这些数据。本应用不对数据进行分析、画像或共享。

**写入哪些数据。** 将上述测量数据写入您手机上的 Android Health Connect。

**数据去向。** 不会发送到任何其他地方。本应用没有服务器、没有账号、没有统计分析、没有广告，除 HUAWEI Health Kit 和 Health Connect 外不含第三方 SDK。数据仅在您设备上的这两个服务之间传递，开发者不会收到您的任何数据。本应用不进行任何数据跨境传输。

**存储。** 本应用仅在设备上保存最近一次同步的时间和结果，不保存测量数据副本。

**您的控制权。**
- 在应用内点击 **Revoke HUAWEI Health access** 撤销华为授权。
- 在 Health Connect 设置 → 应用权限 → Your Health Sync 中移除本应用的权限并删除其写入的数据。
- 随时卸载本应用。

**儿童。** 本应用不面向儿童。

**变更。** 本声明的变更将在本文件中发布。

**联系方式。** 请在 https://github.com/wallwallfacer/your-health-sync/issues 提交 issue。

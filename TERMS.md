# User agreement / 用户协议: Your Health Sync

_Last updated / 更新日期: 2026-11-23_

## English

1. **What it is.** Your Health Sync is a free, open-source Android app (MIT license) that copies your own weight and body-composition measurements from HUAWEI Health to Android Health Connect on the same phone.
2. **Personal, non-commercial use.** The app is provided for personal use. There is no account, no payment and no service operated by the developer.
3. **Your data.** The app only reads data you authorize through HUAWEI Health Kit and only writes it to Health Connect on your device. See the [privacy statement](PRIVACY.md).
4. **Not medical advice.** The app copies numbers; it does not interpret them and is not a medical device.
5. **No warranty.** The app is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. The developer is not liable for lost, wrong or duplicated data. You can remove all data the app wrote from Health Connect settings at any time.
6. **Stopping.** You can revoke access in the app or uninstall it at any time.
7. **Changes.** Changes to this agreement are published in this file in the source repository.
8. **Contact.** https://github.com/wallwallfacer/your-health-sync/issues
